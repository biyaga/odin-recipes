Spaghetti

Spaghetti Carbonara is a traditional Italian pasta dish known for its creamy sauce made from eggs, cheese, and pancetta. This elegant yet simple recipe delivers rich flavor and comfort in just 20 minutes, making it perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or impressive date night meal.

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Eggs and Pecorino Romano cheese
  • Pancetta or guanciale
  1. Cook and prepare - Boil spaghetti until al dente, dice pancetta and cook until crispy, then whisk eggs with grated Pecorino Romano cheese in a bowl.
  2. Combine - Drain pasta reserving 1 cup pasta water, toss hot pasta with crispy pancetta off heat, then quickly stir in egg mixture while adding pasta water gradually until creamy.
  3. Serve - Plate immediately and top with extra Pecorino Romano and cracked black pepper, serve while hot.

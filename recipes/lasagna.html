Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian pasta dish made with layers of noodles, savory meat sauce, and creamy ricotta cheese, all baked until golden and bubbly.

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground meat and tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese and mozzarella cheese
  1. Prepare layers - Cook lasagna noodles, brown ground beef with tomato sauce, and mix ricotta with mozzarella in a bowl.
  2. Assemble - In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture; repeat until full, ending with cheese on top.
  3. Bake - Bake at for 25-30 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly, then let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

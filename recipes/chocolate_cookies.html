Chocolate Cookies

Chocolate Cookies are a beloved classic dessert featuring soft, chewy centers loaded with melted chocolate chunks. With just a few pantry ingredients and simple steps, you can create warm, homemade cookies that rival any bakery treat.

  • Flour, butter, and brown sugar
  • Eggs and vanilla extract
  • Chocolate chips
  1. Mix dry and wet - Cream together butter and brown sugar, beat in eggs and vanilla, then fold in flour mixed with baking soda and salt.
  2. Add chocolate - Stir in chocolate chips, drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet spacing them 2 inches apart.
  3. Bake - Bake at 375'F for 9-11 minutes until golden brown at edges, cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.
